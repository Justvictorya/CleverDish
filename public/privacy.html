CleverDish Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

CleverDish is a free meal-planning tool. It is designed to keep as little data as possible: your plan and food history stay in your own browser unless you choose to sign in.

What stays on your device

Your profile, 28-day meal plan, food and calorie logs, favourites, freezer vault, wallet, badges and settings are saved in your browser's local storage. They are not uploaded to CleverDish. Clearing your browser data removes them, and moving to a different web address starts with an empty set.

What we store when you sign in

If you tap Continue with Google, Google gives us your verified email address, your name and your Google account ID. We keep those in a small free database for two purposes only:

  • to count how many people have signed up, and
  • to let early users know if CleverDish moves to a permanent address.

We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else. You can use the whole app without signing in.

What we do not collect

No passwords. No payment details. No contacts, photos from your device library, precise location, or advertising identifiers.

Third parties

Google Identity Services provides the one-tap sign-in and handles your Google credentials; Google's own privacy policy applies to that interaction. Cloudflare hosts the app and the sign-up database.

Your choices

You can sign in or stay anonymous, clear your browser storage at any time to erase on-device data, and ask for your sign-up record to be deleted using the support email shown on the Google sign-in consent screen.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the support email listed on CleverDish's Google consent screen.
